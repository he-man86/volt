Who owns each region

Volt shell — the window, the title bar, the “IDE changes” button. A thin Electron frame Volt owns.
opencode GUI — the whole agent view, loaded unmodified. It updates with upstream — no fork, no merge cost.
Volt connector — the IDE-changes panel. Rendered by the always-on bridge gateway that already watches the IDE — pure Volt, no opencode.
The one compromise: the panel sits beside the chat, not woven into it. It opens one click away — and the future opencode GUI-plugin moves it back inside the view, still fork-free.
